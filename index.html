The setting

A private retreat, backing to greenspace

Mature landscaping, brick walkways and a private patio frame a home that sits quietly at the edge of the trees — minutes from the Butler Creek Greenway Trails and Butler Creek Park.

The lot measures 9,685 square feet. At its rear boundary, the neighborhood gives way to serene greenspace: no houses behind, just the planted edge of the greenway corridor that runs through this part of Gresham.

The interiors

Soaring ceilings, skylights, room to gather

Expansive windows and skylights carry daylight deep into 2,633 square feet of thoughtfully arranged living space.

A vaulted living room with large picture windows anchors the main floor. The gourmet kitchen holds a center island, granite countertops, stainless appliances, a built-in gas cooktop with downdraft and oak cabinetry — with a bright dining nook that opens by slider to the deck and backyard. A formal dining room and a family room with a brick gas fireplace complete the picture.

Fig. 03The primary suite, main level.

03

The suite

A primary suite that earns the name

Walk-in and reach-in closets, dual vanities, a walk-in shower and its own skylight — the private bath is a quiet finish to the day.

Upstairs, a vaulted bonus room with a built-in desk and attic storage adds a flexible fourth bedroom, office or studio. The main-level laundry includes a utility sink, built-in cabinetry and an ironing station. Central air, gas forced-air heat, a central vacuum system and an attached two-car garage are all in place — and there are no HOA dues.
