Legal-review checklist

Items to resolve with qualified counsel before the promotion opens. This checklist is a planning aid only — it is not legal advice, and completing it does not make the promotion compliant.

  • Confirm whether the proposed promotion constitutes a lottery, raffle, sweepstakes or another regulated promotion.
  • Confirm whether the sponsor is legally eligible to conduct the promotion.
  • Determine whether a free alternative method of entry is required.
  • Determine eligible jurisdictions.
  • Determine state registration/bonding requirements where applicable.
  • Determine advertising/disclosure requirements.
  • Determine tax treatment of the property prize.
  • Determine title-transfer requirements.
  • Determine property insurance requirements.
  • Determine closing-cost responsibility.
  • Determine winner verification requirements.
  • Determine privacy/data-retention requirements.
  • Review payment processor/bank requirements.
  • Review age and residency restrictions.
  • Review official odds disclosure.
  • Review all state-specific restrictions.

This checklist itself does not constitute legal advice, and its completion does not constitute legal approval.

Consumer protection

FTC & consumer disclosures under review

The Federal Trade Commission advises that legitimate prize promotions do not require payment to participate or win; where payment is required for a chance at a prize, the promotion may constitute a lottery under federal and state law. Counsel must review the structure before launch.
