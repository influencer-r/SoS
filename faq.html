IMPORTANT: This promotion is not currently accepting entries. The final entry method, eligibility requirements, payment structure, alternative method of entry, official rules and participating jurisdictions are subject to legal review.

The Haven House Draw is a planned prize promotion centered on a single residential property — 2650 SW 22nd Court, Gresham, Oregon 97080. The intended structure is a property draw with two entry packages. Whether that structure takes the form described here depends on legal review; the final promotion format, its rules, and the method of participation will be published in the Official Rules.
