Privacy

A framework for the Sponsor's privacy policy. Every section below is an outline for attorney review — nothing here is a finished policy, and no personal information is collected by this prototype.

For attorney review

This page is a structural placeholder. It does not describe current data practice, because the prototype does not collect, transmit or store personal information. Each section must be completed with counsel before the promotion opens.

01Information collected

An overview of the categories the finished policy will cover: information provided through the entry form, contact form, and any future account or verification process. The prototype itself collects nothing.

Define categories, sources, and purposes; align with state privacy statutes that may apply to the eligible jurisdictions.

02Account information

[ATTORNEY TO REVIEW / COMPLETE] — whether entrant accounts exist, what they store, and the controls available.

03Contact information

The entry flow is designed to collect full legal name, email address, phone number and state of residence, used to identify participants and contact a drawn winner.

Confirm the minimum-data rationale for each field; decide whether phone is optional; define use limits for marketing (if any).

04Payment information

The website is not designed to receive payment card data. Card numbers, CVV and PIN are never entered on this website, stored by it, or transmitted through it.

Confirm the tokenized/hosted-checkout architecture with the payment provider; document which party is the merchant of record; address any payment-related data the Sponsor will legitimately receive (e.g., confirmation tokens, last-four references).

05Payment processor

Payment is processed by the Sponsor's approved payment provider under its own privacy terms, which will be identified and linked in the finished policy.

Identify the provider, link its privacy terms, and allocate responsibilities (fraud screening, receipts, refunds).
