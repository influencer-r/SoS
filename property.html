Exterior

First impressions

A calm, planted approach to a home that has been thoughtfully maintained since 1993.

The two-car attached garage and brick walkways set the tone; the landscaped grounds — mature plantings, a private patio and the greenway at the rear boundary — carry it through. No HOA dues.

Fig. 02Vaulted living room & picture windows.

02

The main floor

Light as a material

Soaring ceilings, skylights and expansive windows fill the home with natural light.

The vaulted living room's picture windows look straight onto the garden. A formal dining room sits nearby, and the family room gathers around a brick gas fireplace. The main-level laundry room includes a utility sink, built-in cabinetry, workspace and an ironing station.

Fig. 03Gourmet kitchen & center island.

03

The kitchen

The working heart

A center island, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and oak cabinetry — with a gas cooktop that vents itself.

The built-in gas cooktop has downdraft ventilation; the built-in wall oven, dishwasher and refrigerator are all newer additions. A pantry keeps the counters clear, oak floors run underfoot, and a bright dining nook opens by slider to the deck and backyard — the indoor-outdoor hinge of the house.

Fig. 04Primary suite.

04

The primary suite

Rooms that end the day well

Both walk-in and reach-in closets, and a private bath with dual vanities, a walk-in shower and its own skylight.

Upstairs, the bonus room adds a vaulted ceiling, a ceiling fan, a built-in desk and attic storage — flexible space for a fourth bedroom, office or studio. Additional bedrooms and a dedicated office/den round out the plan.
