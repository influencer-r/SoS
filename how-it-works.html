The process

Six steps, explained plainly

How the Haven House Draw is intended to work, end to end. The final process depends on the published Official Rules.

Not currently accepting entries

IMPORTANT: This promotion is not currently accepting entries. The final entry method, eligibility requirements, payment structure, alternative method of entry, official rules and participating jurisdictions are subject to legal review.

Step by step

From first look to verified winner

01

Review eligibility

Confirm you meet the eligibility requirements in the Official Rules — age of majority, residency and jurisdiction limits, and household restrictions. Until the rules are final, treat eligibility as undecided.

02

Review the property

Explore the property profile, the gallery and the aerial film. Prize details, title conditions and any inspection rights will be defined in the final rules.

03

Choose your entry

When open, choose between the two entry packages — one entry for $5 or five entries for $25. These are the only packages the site offers.

04

Submit your information

Provide your full legal name, email and phone so the sponsor can reach you if your entry is drawn, plus your state of residence for eligibility. Details are handled per the Privacy policy.

05

Complete the approved payment process

Payment runs through the sponsor's approved payment provider and its secure, hosted checkout. Card details are never entered on this website. An entry counts only after the provider confirms payment.

06

Drawing and winner verification

After the entry period closes, a drawing is conducted as specified in the Official Rules, and the provisional winner completes identity and eligibility verification before any prize is awarded.

A

The dates

Key dates

The intended entry period runs approximately one month, closing on a lawyer-approved date published in the final rules.

The countdown on the home page reflects a projected closing date used for planning only. When the Official Rules are final, that variable is updated once and the countdown reflects the official deadline. When the entry period closes, the countdown stops, the entry interfaces close automatically, and the closing message appears. The countdown does not reset on repeat visits.

B

The integrity of the process

What a fair draw requires

The specifics are being drafted with counsel. The intent, stated plainly:

  • Documented drawing procedure — conducted as specified in the final rules.
  • Winner verification — identity, eligibility and any required documentation before award.
  • Clear odds disclosure — odds depend on the number of valid entries received, as the final rules will specify.
  • Alternative method of entry — whether a no-purchase route exists is a core legal-review question, to be determined and published.
  • No card data on this site — the website never collects card numbers; payment runs through the approved provider only.
